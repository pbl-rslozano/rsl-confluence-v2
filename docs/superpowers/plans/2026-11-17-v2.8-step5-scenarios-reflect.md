# v2.8 Step 5 — Scenarios (WP22) and Chart reflect view (WP24) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add the collapsed **Scenarios (simulation)** section to the detail sheet: a direction-neutral candle bootstrap with range bands, three scenario paths with odds from the coin's own past, and a rule-based explanation. It can be drawn on the chart as hollow simulated candles with Play. Add **Heikin-Ashi** and **Reflect view** as opt-in chart styles. Record the official 6-bar bands and odds on every ledger row (`scb`, `scp`), and add a collapsed **Scenario check** in the ledger dialog.

**Architecture:**
- **Pure core.** `lib/scenarios.ts` holds the simulation, the odds and the ledger fields. `lib/scenario-explain.ts` builds the explanation as i18n keys with parameters. `lib/chart-geometry.ts` holds the chart maths: bucketing, Heikin-Ashi, the projection slots, the Play reducer and the one price y-mapping. `lib/scenario-check.ts` holds the ledger statistics. None of them does I/O.
- **Sheet.** The section runs the simulation in the existing report worker (`report.worker.ts`, new message kind), with the same main-thread fallback the report has. `components/scenario-panel.tsx` renders the section and hands a `ChartProjection` to `PriceChart` through its new `projection` prop.
- **Official run.** The scan path (`scan-record.ts`, H = 6, the last 1,000 bars ending at each row's own bar) writes `scb`/`scp` where `ft` becomes final. Live rows get them in `writeLiveRows`, catch-up rows in `enrichCatchUp`, and the recorder applies them after its Fund trend step. All three use the same `applyScenarios`.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 15 / React 19 (static export), TypeScript run by `node --experimental-strip-types` (`scripts/run-engine-tests.mjs`), custom SVG chart, Web Worker, Node 22 recorder, Playwright acceptance (`tools/e2e/acceptance.cjs`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-v2.8-architecture-design.md` rev 13: §WP22 (lines ~297–356), §WP24 (~358–376), §WP21 (Fund trend state, ~258–295), §3 (design unchanged, allowed items 1, 2 and 4), §4 (checks and docs) and §5 (left to the builder). WP23 (Confluence v2) is out of scope. Do not add a second engine profile, hysteresis or a "Confluence v2" line.

- **Simulation (verbatim).**
  - **200 paths.** Block bootstrap with **blocks of 6 candles**.
  - Blocks are drawn from bars whose volatility (ATR(14)/close percentile) is **within ±15 points** of today's. When too few blocks qualify, widen to **±30**, then use **all bars**, and say so in the explanation (§5).
  - Each past candle becomes a relative candle (open, high, low, close ÷ previous close).
  - **Direction-neutral:** the sample's mean log return is removed.
  - Horizons **6 bars** and **24 bars**, switchable.
  - **Seeded from pair + timeframe + last closed bar time.**
  - **Range bands** at each step: **2.5–97.5 %** (light) and **16–84 %** (darker) of the simulated closes; the **median is dotted**.
  - **Minimum 300 bars**, else the section says "not enough history".
- **Odds (verbatim).**
  - **State** = the Fund trend direction only: **down ≤ −15 · flat · up ≥ 15**.
  - **Matches** are past bars in that state, **sampled every 2 bars**.
  - **n_eff = matches × 2 ÷ H, and it must be ≥ 20.** Otherwise use all bars, shown as "Not enough similar moments — showing this coin's overall odds."
  - **Outcome** after H bars in units of **σ_H = ATR(14)/close × √H at the signal bar** (log-return units):
    - **Continue > +0.5** in the trend's direction, **Reverse < −0.5**, **Pause** otherwise;
    - with a flat trend: **Up / Range / Down**.
  - Each scenario shows its % with a **Wilson 95 %** range computed on **n_eff**, the cases, n_eff, and the coin's **all-bars base rate**.
  - **Scenario path** = among the 200 paths that end in that bucket, the one nearest the bucket median. An empty bucket draws no candles, and its odds still show.
- **Graphic (verbatim).**
  - It appears only after the section is opened and **Show on chart** is tapped.
  - The time axis gains **H empty slots**, and the bands fill them.
  - Candles are **hollow with a dashed outline**. A **"SIMULATED"** label sits on the area, and the axis marks **"now"**.
  - Tabs **Continue / Pause / Reverse** switch the path.
  - **▶ Play** draws the candles **one at a time (~250 ms each)** and stops at H. Pause and Replay are offered. With reduced motion, all candles appear at once.
  - It works with zoom, pan, fullscreen and Heikin-Ashi. Chart replay is not changed.
  - Turning it off restores the exact v2.7 chart.
  - Performance: at most **2 band polygons and 24 simulated candles** are drawn (§5).

## Review Focus

1. **The same view must give the same numbers on every path.**
   - Cases: the worker and the main-thread fallback; two opens of the same coin; the recorder in Node and the app in a WebView (`Math.log`/`Math.exp` can differ in the last ulp across engines).
   - Expected: bands, odds and the chosen paths are identical for the same bars, seed and daily bars. Stored values are rounded, so the app and recorder rows match.
   - Test owners: Tasks 2, 5 and 7.
2. **Short, gapped or partly-stateless history.**
   - Cases: 299 vs 300 bars; a missing bar inside the series; the 1M timeframe (months of 28–31 days); a daily series that starts less than 365 days before the first sheet bar; a non-crypto row; the hosted build (no daily bars); a stale daily cache.
   - Expected: "not enough history", or the overall-odds fallback with its reason. A relative candle, a block or an outcome window never bridges a gap, and nothing is ever NaN or a throw.
   - Test owners: Tasks 1, 2 and 5.
3. **The official 1,000-bar run versus the sheet's 5K history.**
   - Cases: a row whose stored series is 3,000 bars; a catch-up row in the middle of the series.
   - Expected: the official fields always use the 1,000 bars ending at that row's own bar. ATR is computed on that window, because Wilder's ATR is recursive and depends on where the window starts. A longer stored history never changes a recorded row. The sheet says "The recorded check uses the last 1,000 bars." whenever it holds more.
   - Test owners: Tasks 3 and 7.
4. **Heikin-Ashi, Reflect and the projection, combined with zoom, pan and fullscreen.**
   - Expected: the mirror is exact and bodies never collapse to 1 px. Simulated Heikin-Ashi continues from the last real Heikin-Ashi candle. When zoomed out, simulated candles are bucketed (≤ 24 drawn). Turning everything off gives the same SVG as before.

5. **200 × 24 paths and Play must not jank, and a late result must never show for another coin.**

### Task 1: Candle bootstrap simulation (pure)

**Files:**
- Create: `apps/confluence/web/src/lib/scenarios.ts`
- Test: `apps/confluence/web/src/lib/scenarios.test.mjs` (script `test:scenarios`, added to `test`)

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `atr(k, 14)` (Wilder) from `indicators.ts`, and `rng(seed)` (mulberry32) from `ledger-metrics.ts`.
- Produces:
  - **Constants:** `SC_PATHS = 200`, `SC_BLOCK = 6`, `SC_STEPS = 24`, `SC_HORIZONS = [6, 24] as const`, `type ScHorizon = 6 | 24`, `SC_MIN_BARS = 300`, `SC_ATR = 14`, `SC_VOL_BAND = 15`, `SC_VOL_WIDE = 30`, `SC_MIN_BLOCKS = 20`, `SC_QUANTILES = [0.025, 0.16, 0.5, 0.84, 0.975] as const`.
  - `interface Ohlc { open: number; high: number; low: number; close: number }`.
  - `scenarioSeed(pair: string, tf: string, lastBarTime: number): number`: FNV-1a 32-bit (offset 2166136261, prime 16777619, `Math.imul`, `>>> 0`) over the UTF-16 code units of `` `${pair}|${tf}|${lastBarTime}` ``.
  - `quantile7(sorted: ArrayLike<number>, q: number): number`: linear interpolation at `(n − 1)·q` (R type 7).
  - `stepOk(prevTime: number, time: number, intervalMs: number): boolean`: `0 < Δ ≤ maxSpan`.
    - `maxSpan = 31 days` when `intervalMs ≥ 28 days`, else `1.5 × intervalMs`.
    - This is the same contiguity notion as `measureOutcome`.
  - `volPercentiles(bars: Kline[]): { atr: number[]; pct: number[] }`.
    - `atr` = Wilder ATR(14) on exactly these bars.
    - `pct[i]` = percentile rank 0–100 of `atr[i]/close[i]` among all finite values: `100 × (#below + 0.5 × #equal) / n`.
    - NaN where ATR is not defined.
  - `interface Band { p025: number; p16: number; p50: number; p84: number; p975: number }` (prices).
  - `interface Simulation { paths: Ohlc[][]; bands: Band[]; volPct: number; widened: 15 | 30 | "all"; blocks: number; mu: number }`.
    - `paths.length === 200`, each 24 candles.
    - `bands[s − 1]` is step s, s = 1…24.
  - `simulate(bars: Kline[], intervalMs: number, seed: number): Simulation | null`. It returns null when `bars.length < 300` or no block is eligible.
  - Test hook: `eligibleStartsForTests(bars: Kline[], intervalMs: number, band: 15 | 30 | "all"): number[]`.
- Algorithm. The tests do not determine this, so it is pinned here:
  1. **Relative candle.** `rel[i] = (o_i, h_i, l_i, c_i) ÷ c_{i−1}` for i ≥ 1. It is valid only when `stepOk(t_{i−1}, t_i)` holds and every value is finite and > 0.
  2. **Block start.** A block start `j` is eligible when `rel[j…j+5]` are all valid and `pct[j − 1]` (the bar whose close starts the block) lies within ±15 of `pct[last]`.
  3. **Widening.** With fewer than 20 eligible starts, use ±30. With still fewer than 20, use every start with valid `rel`.
  4. **μ.** μ = mean of `ln(rel.c)` over the distinct relative candles covered by the eligible blocks. Every drawn relative candle is multiplied by `exp(−μ)`, all four fields.
  5. **Paths.** Drawing is path-major: path 0, steps 1…24, then path 1, and so on. At each step `s % 6 === 0`, draw `j = starts[Math.floor(r() · starts.length)]`. Step s uses `rel[j + s % 6]`, scaled to the running close `P` (which starts at the last close). Then `P` = the new close.
  6. **Bands.** At each step, sort the 200 closes and take `quantile7` at the five quantiles.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests.** Fixtures: `series(n, {drift, vol, seed, t0, intervalMs})` builds a deterministic GBM-like random walk from `rng`.
  - `seed is FNV-1a of pair|tf|time`: `scenarioSeed("BTCUSDT","1h",1700000000000)` equals the value computed by an inline reference loop in the test. A different time gives a different seed.
  - `299 bars → null, 300 bars → simulation`: `simulate(series(299)) === null`. `simulate(series(300)).paths.length === 200`, every path has 24 candles, and `bands.length === 24`.
  - `same bars and seed → identical simulation`: `deepEqual(simulate(b,…,s), simulate(structuredClone(b),…,s))`. A different seed gives different `paths[0]`.
  - `quantiles are type 7 and bands are ordered`: `quantile7([1,2,3,4], 0.16) ≈ 1.48`. Every band satisfies `p025 ≤ p16 ≤ p50 ≤ p84 ≤ p975`.
  - `direction-neutral: a strong uptrend gives a centred fan`:
    - The series drifts +1 % per bar with volatility 1 %.
    - The mean of `ln(P_24/P_0)` over the 200 paths is within ±0.25 × its standard deviation of 0.
    - `bands[23].p50` is within 2 % of the last close.
  - `candles stay consistent`: every simulated candle has `low ≤ min(open, close)` and `high ≥ max(open, close)`. `paths[p][0].open / lastClose` equals the drawn `rel.o × exp(−μ)` (checked through one path).
  - `a gap is never bridged`:
    - A 600-bar series with one missing bar at index 400.
    - No eligible block contains relative candle 400. Assert through an exported test hook `eligibleStartsForTests(bars, intervalMs, band): number[]`: no start j with `j ≤ 400 ≤ j + 5`.
  - `too few bars in today's band widens to ±30, then all`:
    - The series is calm for 290 bars, then 10 wild bars, so today's percentile is about 99 → `widened === 30` or `"all"` as the fixture dictates.
    - A uniform series → `widened === 15`.
  - `1M bars of 28–31 days are contiguous`: a monthly series with calendar-month times → `simulate` is not null.

**Files:**

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1; `fundTrendSeries`, `roundFt`, `FT_MAX_STALE_MS`, and `wilson(successes, n)` (z = 1.96) from `ledger-metrics.ts`.
- Produces:
  - **Constants:** `SC_STATE_TH = 15`, `SC_SAMPLE_EVERY = 2`, `SC_MIN_NEFF = 20`, `SC_BUCKET = 0.5`, `SC_OFFICIAL_H = 6`.
  - `type FtState = "up" | "flat" | "down"`. `ftState(ft: number | null | undefined): FtState | null` maps ≤ −15 → down, ≥ 15 → up, otherwise flat. Null and non-finite → null.
  - `barClose(openTime: number, intervalMs: number): number` is a local copy of `market-client.barCloseTime`. That module pulls in venue code, which is why `fund-trend.ts` keeps its own `dailyTail`. Pin it with a test.
  - `ftAtBars(daily: Kline[] | null, bars: Kline[], intervalMs: number): (number | null)[]`. It is aligned to `bars` and uses `fundTrendSeries` (no look-ahead).
    - Each value is `roundFt(v)`.
    - The value is null when there is no value, or when the newest daily bar used closed more than `FT_MAX_STALE_MS` before that bar's close. This is the same rule as `applyFundTrend`, so `ftAtBars(...)[last] === row.ft`.
  - `type Bucket = "up" | "range" | "down"`, `BUCKETS = ["up", "range", "down"] as const`. `bucketOf(x: number): Bucket` gives up when x > 0.5, down when x < −0.5, otherwise range.
  - `type Scenario = "continue" | "pause" | "reverse" | "up" | "range" | "down"`.
    - `scenarioOf(b: Bucket, s: FtState | null): Scenario`. In an up trend: up → continue, range → pause, down → reverse. A down trend is the mirror. Flat or null → up / range / down.
