# v2.8 Step 4a — Market radar core (WP25, part 1) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add the Radar: a right-edge tab, collapsed by default, that opens a panel with Positioning, Buyer pressure,
Regional demand, Institutions, Alerts and Data sources & thanks. It uses free sources only and is context, never a vote.
Radar hosts are contacted only while the panel is open or while **Record radar context** (default off) is on, in which
case ledger rows (app and recorder) carry `rad`.

**Scope split (ruling, 16 Nov 2026):** WP25 is built in two PRs.
- **4a (this plan):** everything over plain REST from free hosts.
- **4b (next plan):**
  - live liquidations: Binance `!forceOrder@arr` and Bybit `allLiquidation` websockets in the app and the recorder, with the 30-day hourly baseline and the liquidation-cascade alert;
  - gateway provider profiles (provider-key kind, one `verifyCredentials` test call per provider): Whale Alert large transfers and one ETF-flow provider.

  In 4a the paid rows already show "Available with a key on the desktop gateway". The liquidation row and the cascade alert are absent.

**Architecture:**
- Pure radar logic lives in `apps/confluence/web/src/lib/radar/*.ts`, with one module per section plus `alerts.ts`. Fetchers sit beside the pure functions, take an injected `fetch`/clock, use the venue rate gates and honour cooldowns.
- `lib/radar/snapshot.ts` assembles one `RadarSnapshot` on a cadence and keeps a per-device snapshot log. It also derives the ledger's `RadarContext`, with no look-ahead: the newest snapshot with `t ≤ tc` and age ≤ 2 h, like `derivativeFields`.
- The UI is a portal-mounted tab and sheet (`components/radar-panel.tsx`). The recorder reuses the same lib code through `recorder/src/engine.mjs`.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js static export, TypeScript, Radix Sheet (`components/ui/sheet.tsx`), Node test scripts (`scripts/run-engine-tests.mjs`), Playwright acceptance.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-v2.8-architecture-design.md` (rev 12): WP25, §3 item 3 (Record radar context) and item 8 (Radar tab), §4 RD-* checks. Probe evidence (16 Nov 2026, built-in browser):
- `fapi.binance.com` answers with `access-control-allow-origin: *`.
- `publicreporting.cftc.gov` and `stablecoins.llama.fi` are CORS-OK.
- DefiLlama's hacks endpoint is documented as Pro-only, so it is linked, never fetched.

## Global Constraints

- **Context, never a vote:**
  - Radar values never enter scoring. Engine config id `5c30b5c39f5ba3f2` and `ENGINE_VERSION` 2.4.0 are unchanged.
  - No radar alert blocks or places a trade. The WP19 depeg paper gate (≥ 1 %) is separate and unchanged.
  - Paper trading only, with no order endpoint (R12).
- **Fetch gating:**
  - Radar hosts are contacted only while the Radar panel is open, or while `recordRadar` (default **false**) is on.
  - Otherwise no radar request is made, timers are stopped, and `rad` is null.
  - The default build contacts no radar host: BLD-9.4-3a stays green unchanged.
- **New hosts (ADR-0003 amendment + R8 + egress manifest):**
  - All three are app CSP-direct and also recorder hosts: `https://fapi.binance.com` (REST only), `https://publicreporting.cftc.gov`, `https://stablecoins.llama.fi`.
  - No `wss://` in 4a.
  - Existing hosts are reused for Coinbase, Upbit (relay), Coins.ph (relay), Binance spot, Bybit, Bitget and OKX.
- **Binance USDⓈ-M futures is a rate venue, not a scan source:**
  - Add venue `"binance-fut"` (own cooldown and gate, not the spot pool) to `Venue`, `rateLimitedUntil` and `venueOfUrl`.
  - Do not add it to `DataSource`, `DATA_SOURCES`, `IMPORT_SOURCES` or the gateway rules.
- **Subject:**
  - The radar describes the market through **BTC**: Positioning shows BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT perps; regional premiums, break-away and volume spike use BTC; Institutions covers CME BTC (133741) and ETH (146021).
  - `rad` records the BTC values.
- **Cadence while allowed:**
  - Positioning, regional premiums, buyer pressure and break-away: every **5 min**.
  - Daily inputs (30-day standing premiums, volume history, stablecoin supply): once per UTC day, cached.
  - CFTC: at most once per **12 h**, cached.
  - Every request goes through its venue gate at ≤ 80 % of the documented limit and stops for that venue on 429/418.
- **Starting thresholds** (labelled "starting values", adjustable in the panel, stored per device):
  - break-away ≥ **1 %** for ≥ **2** closed 1h bars against the median of **≥ 2** other venues;
  - volume spike z ≥ **3** against 30 days;
  - stablecoin supply jump: |daily change| above the **99th** percentile of the prior 90 days.
  - Depeg uses the existing `DEPEG_TIERS` (0.5 / 1 / 2 %).
- **Standing premium:** Coins.ph (PHP) and Upbit (KRW) are compared after removing their own median premium over the last **30** closed daily bars. A venue with fewer than **20** of those days is left out of break-away, and the panel says so.
- **Korea premium (spec, verbatim):** Upbit `KRW-BTC` ÷ (Upbit `KRW-USDT` × Binance BTCUSDT) − 1. The panel says it leaves out the won's own premium on USDT. US premium = Coinbase BTC-USD ÷ Binance BTC-USDT − 1. PH premium = `phPremiumPct`.
- **CFTC (spec):**
  - Traders in Financial Futures, futures only (Socrata dataset `gpe5-46if`), contract codes `133741` (BTC) and `146021` (ETH).
  - Asset managers vs leveraged funds as groups: long, short and net, with the change vs the prior report.
  - Shown with the report date ("positions as of Tuesday, usually published Friday 3:30 p.m. ET").
- **ETF flows (4a):** imported CSV only (`date,issuer,flow_usd`). The live provider row says "Available with a key on the desktop gateway".
- **UI (§3 item 8, owner-approved):**
  - Right-edge tab "Radar", collapsed by default.
  - The panel opens over the list as a right side sheet at ≥ **1024 px** and a bottom sheet below that.
  - Key **R** toggles it (ignored in inputs, textareas, selects, contenteditable and while another dialog is open); **Esc** closes it.
  - Open state and section collapse are remembered per device (`localStorage` `ct-radar-v1`). `prefers-reduced-motion` means no slide animation.
  - Sections, each collapsible: Positioning · Buyer pressure · Regional demand · Institutions · Alerts · Data sources & thanks.
  - DS-1/DS-2 stay green: the tab is portal-mounted outside `header`, `main` and `footer`.
- **Ledger `rad`:**
  - Logged, never scored. Nullable per field.
  - CSV columns are appended after `fund_trend`. WP22's `scb`/`scp` go after these (ruling).
  - The sanitiser rejects bad types and ranges. Alert codes must match `[A-Z0-9_]{1,32}`, at most 16 per row.
- **Repo rules (CLAUDE.md):**
  - Never delete an acceptance check; `packages/core` untouched.
  - No organisation or contact details.
  - Exact pinned versions, with no new dependencies unless unavoidable.
  - Tier-2 catalogs are never CERTIFIED. New keys go into all 18 catalogs, Arabic drafts in `@pendingReview`, ICU vars in VARS (`scripts/validate-catalogs.mjs`).

## Review Focus

1. **A source is down, rate-limited or partly answers while the panel is open:**
   - Its rows show "unavailable" with the time of the last good value. The value is never shown as fresh, and other sections still render.
   - A missing venue never produces a break-away. That needs ≥ 2 other venues with data.
   - Test: Task 4 `breakaway needs two other venues`; Task 5 `stale values are marked`.
2. **Panel closed and `recordRadar` off:** zero radar requests, including timers left running after closing and the R key toggling quickly.
   - Test: Task 5 `no fetch when not allowed`; acceptance RD-1 and RD-6.
3. **Thin history:**
   - Funding with fewer than 90 samples, OI history shorter than 25 h, or a premium venue with fewer than 20 daily closes shows "not enough history" or is excluded, never 0.
   - Tests in Tasks 1, 2 and 4.
4. **CFTC or DefiLlama shape drift:** a missing or renamed field, a non-numeric string or an empty array gives "n/a" for that item, never NaN or a crash.
   - Test: Task 3 `cftc rows with missing fields`, `supply series with gaps`.
5. **Hostile imports:**
   - ETF CSV: formula-like cells, huge numbers, bad dates and duplicate rows are rejected per row, with counts reported.
   - Ledger CSV: `radar_alerts` with `|=cmd` or lower-case codes is rejected.
   - Tests in Tasks 3 and 7.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:**
  - `no fetch when not allowed`: `radarAllowed` false for {closed, off}, true for open or record. A runner that is stopped makes 0 fetcher calls across 3 timer ticks. Start, stop and start again within 1 s gives no duplicate timers.
