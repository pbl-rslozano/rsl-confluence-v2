## 1. Intent

Ronald uses the Terminal to judge crypto setups and practise in paper trading. The audits showed the app *measures* edge
like a professional but *builds* signals like a retail dashboard, has softer loss limits than any prop firm, and shows
pattern "bias" that is mostly noise. v2.8 makes it honest and safe first, then lets him **see** the difference between
ordinary indicators and how trend funds trade, and **see** possible next moves as simulated candles with odds and
reasons — without changing the screens he already knows.

**Success:** (1) at 10% below its peak the paper account stops trading and closes its trades (gaps, moves between bar closes, exit costs and the wait for each position's own next bar
open can take
it further), and no lock can be loosened while it is active;
(2) the Pattern column abstains on noise; (3) Fund trend and Scenarios are one tap away and clearly labelled "not a
forecast"; (4) the ledger can later tell whether Scenarios' odds and range bands were right (coverage, Brier score);
(5) with default settings the layout matches v2.7 except for the §3 allowed additions (checked by DS-1/DS-2).

**Anti-goals (a failure even if it works):** simulations that read as predictions; anything that blinks without a way to
stop it; a changed default layout; new voting indicators; any AI-written text in the explanations; a network host other than those named in WP25, WP26, WP28 and WP29, or one
added without its ADR-0003 amendment, R8 entry and build-time check.

### WP19 — Safety and honesty fixes (strategy audit)

Paper risk (`risk-engine.ts`, `paper-broker.ts`):
- **Drawdown lock (owner decision 15 Nov):** `RiskSettings.maxDrawdownLockPct` (default 10, range 1–50).
  `PaperAccount.peakEquity` (stored; `PAPER_SCHEMA` 2 with a migration; passed in `AccountState`) = highest equity incl.
  open P&L, checked after each `processBars` call with every position at its latest mark (every scan; bars
  missed while the app was closed are marked on the next scan from their real closes). When equity ≤ peak × (1 − pct) the
  lock (a) blocks new trades (`paper.rule.drawdownLock`), (b) cancels pending positions and (c) **closes every open trade
  at the next open of its own series** (via `closeRequestedAt`; a position whose series is not being scanned closes at
  that series' next scan, and the dead-man badge shows it),
  reason "drawdown lock" (gaps, moves between marks, exit costs and a longer timeframe's wait for its next open can take the loss past 10%;
  the guide says so). Only while the drawdown lock is active and after every lock close has filled (no open or pending
  positions), typed **RESET** (same word as the kill
  switch, separate button; while closes are still waiting, the button names the series to scan so they fill) sets the
  peak to current equity — a fresh 10% — and is journaled; the no-loosening rule starts
  again from the next UTC day. "Start of day/week" equity = the first mark at or after 00:00 UTC each day / Monday.
  Prop-firm reference: Topstep closes positions at its Maximum Loss Limit, which trails the end-of-day balance; FTMO's
  Maximum Loss is 10% of initial capital measured on equity (2-Step; on the 1-Step the floor trails the highest
  end-of-day balance). This lock trails the highest marked equity (stricter), and
  Topstep's paid Reset restarts a Trading Combine at its starting balance; this RESET keeps the loss and only restarts the
  10% from current equity.
- **Locks can't be loosened while active:** `updateSettings` refuses increases to loss locks, risk per trade, open risk,
  leverage while the daily, weekly or drawdown lock or the kill switch is active (not the dead-man badge)

  tighter stops; the stop is placed from the filled entry, so this sizing is slightly conservative: a clean stop-out

### WP21 — Fund trend (overlay, ribbon, panel, rank)

**Method** (adapted from Moskowitz–Ooi–Pedersen 2012 and Hurst–Ooi–Pedersen 2017, which trade the **sign** of each
horizon's past return; here a clipped volatility-scaled return replaces the sign so it can share the score's axis), on
**closed daily bars**:
- returns over 30, 91 and 365 days (crypto trades every day; ≈ 1, 3, 12 months);
- daily volatility σ: EWMA of squared daily log returns (not demeaned, unlike MOP), centre of mass 60 days (δ = 60/61), using bars up to t−1;
  annualised σ = σ·√365 (MOP use √261 for trading days);
- per horizon k: zₖ = ln(Cₜ / Cₜ₋ₖ) / (σ·√k), clipped to ±2; **Fund trend** = 50 × mean(zₖ) → −100…+100;
- **Fund size** = min(200%, 40% ÷ annualised σ) — what a fund targeting 40% yearly volatility per asset would hold,
  as % of normal size;
- needs ≥ 366 closed daily bars, else "not enough history"; daily klines fetched from the row's own source (same hosts,
  limit 1,000, weight 2), cached per UTC day per pair, closed bars only;
- **which rows [builder-check call]:** crypto rows only (spot, perps and Coins.ph peso pairs from their own daily bars);
  tokenised stocks, FX and commodities show "n/a" (their weekend prices are synthetic). The value does not depend on the
  row's timeframe. Budget: one daily request per crypto pair per UTC day (≈ 100–200 pairs × weight 2 = 200–400 of 6,000/min on
  Binance), queued behind the scan with the existing pacing and 429/418 halt; the recorder does the same.
- Intraday bars take the value computed from daily bars **closed at or before that bar's close** (no look-ahead).

**On screen** (all off by default):
- **Ribbon** (Indicators menu): thin band at the bottom of the price pane; green up / red down / grey |x| < 15; opacity
  rises with |x|.
- **Panel** (Indicators menu): Confluence score and Fund trend on one −100…+100 axis; Fund size as a shaded area.
  **Per-bar score [builder-check call]:** the visible bars (at most 300) whose full 1,000-bar (`ENGINE_BARS`) window — the bar and the 999 closed bars before it — is
  available are re-scored in the scan worker
  (the `runCatchUp` path in `scan-jobs.ts`) with the
  production 1,000-bar window, cached per pair, timeframe and last bar; ledger rows
  are reused where they exist (same cfg, wb = 1,000). 300 scored bars need ≥ 1,299 contiguous bars: with fewer, the panel
  fetches the missing older bars once (same host, one history page) or draws the line only where a full window exists and
  says so. Scored bars use exactly the list's score; other bars show no line.
- **Disagreement:** Confluence BUY/SELL (|score| ≥ 55) against a Fund trend of the opposite sign with |x| ≥ 25 →

### WP22 — Scenarios (simulation with candlesticks)

New pure modules `lib/scenarios.ts` (simulation, scenario odds) and `lib/scenario-explain.ts` (rule-based text); run in the
existing report worker; rendered by `components/scenario-panel.tsx` and a new optional `projection` prop of `PriceChart`.

**History used:** the row's own closed bars on the current timeframe (the 1,000 the sheet has, plus any loaded with
"Load 5K"). Minimum 300 bars, else the section says "not enough history".

**Candle bootstrap (the simulation):**
- each past candle becomes a relative candle (open, high, low, close ÷ previous close);
- **200 paths**, each H steps, built by a **block bootstrap** (blocks of 6 candles, so calm and wild stretches stay
  together), drawing blocks from bars whose volatility (ATR(14)/close percentile) is within ±15 points of today's;
- **direction-neutral:** the sample's mean log return is removed, so the fan says how far, never which way;
- horizons: **6 bars** (matches the Pattern and ledger horizon) and **24 bars**, switchable;
- **reproducible:** seeded from pair + timeframe + last closed bar time (same view → same simulation);
- **range bands** at each step: 2.5–97.5% (light) and 16–84% (darker) of simulated closes; median dotted.

**Three scenarios (odds from the coin's own past):**
- **state [builder-check call — with 1,000 bars there are only ~165 non-overlapping 6-bar windows]:** the Fund trend
  direction only (**down ≤ −15 · flat · up ≥ 15**); volatility already shapes the range bands, so it is not crossed in;
  each past bar needs the Fund trend of its own day, so the daily series must reach 365 days before the first sheet bar
  (one 1,000-bar request covers 1h–12h sheets of 1,000 bars; for 1d and longer, and after "Load 5K", older daily bars
  are paged with the existing history fetch, or bars without a state are left out and only the rest count in n_eff);
- matches: past bars in that state, sampled every 2 bars (overlapping windows allowed); the **effective sample**
  n_eff = matches × 2 ÷ H must be **≥ 20**. If not, fall back to **all bars** (the base rate itself) and say so
  ("not enough similar moments — showing this coin's overall odds"). At H = 24 this usually needs "Load 5K";
- outcome after H bars, in units of σ_H = ATR(14)/close × √H at the signal bar (log-return units; ATR-based, not a standard deviation): **Continue** > +0.5 in the trend's direction, **Reverse** < −0.5,
  **Pause** otherwise; when the trend is flat: **Up / Range / Down**;
- shown as % with a Wilson 95% range computed on **n_eff** (not the overlapping match count), cases and n_eff, and the
  coin's **all-bars base rate** beside it;
- **scenario path:** among the 200 simulated paths whose end falls in that scenario's bucket, the one nearest the bucket
  median; if a bucket has no simulated path, no candles are drawn for it (the odds still show).

**Graphic (only after the user opens the section and taps "Show on chart"):**
- the chart's time axis extends by H empty slots to the right of the last real candle; bands fill them;
- the selected scenario's candles are **hollow with a dashed outline**, a "SIMULATED" label sits on the area, the axis
  marks "now"; tabs **Continue / Pause / Reverse** switch paths;
- **▶ Play** draws the candles one at a time (~250 ms each), stops at H; Pause/Replay; reduced motion → all at once;
- works in zoom, pan, fullscreen and with **Heikin-Ashi** (simulated candles converted the same way; when zoomed out,
  bucketed candles are built the same way); Chart replay is not changed;
- turning it off restores the exact v2.7 chart.

**Explanation (rule-based, i18n keys with parameters, 18 languages):** one short paragraph built from: Fund trend
state; Confluence label and which bloc disagrees; volatility regime; the invalidation level (2 × ATR(14) against the
trend, or the last swing high/low over 20 bars, whichever is nearer); the next macro event inside the horizon; funding
/ basis when the row is a perp; and the sample-size caveat. Fixed header: **"Possible scenarios from past data — not a
forecast."**

**Which run is official [builder-check call]:** the scan path (`scan-record.ts`, fixed 1,000-bar window, **H = 6**) is
the official one; the recorder and catch-up rows compute the same fields from the same data. The sheet uses its own
history (incl. "Load 5K") and says "the recorded check uses the last 1,000 bars" when they differ.

**Self-check (ledger):** each scanned row records the 6-bar band edges (16/84 and 2.5/97.5) and the three scenario
probabilities (`scb`, `scp` fields; CSV columns + sanitiser). When outcomes close, a collapsed **Scenario check**
section in the ledger dialog shows band coverage (target 68% / 95%, with Wilson range) and the three-category Brier
score (sum over outcomes) of the
scenario odds against the base-rate odds.

**Heikin-Ashi** (Indicators menu, display only, default off): HA candles for real and simulated bars; scores, levels
and the readout keep using real prices (readout shows both).

### WP24 — Chart reflect view (display only)

**Why:** a falling market is harder to read than a rising one for many people; traders check a chart's symmetry by flipping
it (TradingView calls this "invert scale"). **What:** an *Indicators* menu toggle **Reflect view** (default off; keyboard
**I** on PC) that mirrors the price pane vertically: the price axis runs high-to-low, so a fall is drawn as a rise.
- Candles keep their **real colours and real prices** (a falling candle stays red; axis labels and the readout show
  real prices in their real order of value); only the drawing is mirrored. MACD and volume panels are not flipped; the
  Fund trend panel is flipped together with price so the two stay comparable.
- A clear **"Reflected"** badge sits on the price pane (and in fullscreen and screenshots), so a reflected chart is
  never mistaken for a real uptrend; the toggle remembers its state per device like the other layers.
- Works with zoom, pan, fullscreen, Heikin-Ashi, the Fund trend ribbon and the Scenarios simulation (simulated candles
  and bands are mirrored the same way); turning it off restores the exact v2.7 drawing.
- Implementation: one y-mapping function in `price-chart.tsx` (`y = reflect ? PAD_T + (price − lo)·k : PAD_T + (hi −
  price)·k`) used by every price-pane layer; shapes between two prices (candle bodies, band fills) use `Math.min`/`Math.abs` of the
  two y values (today's body uses y(max(o,c)) as its top and would collapse to 1 px); the crosshair stays on the hovered
  candle's close and the readout keeps its real OHLC.
- Tests: unit test that the mapping is an exact mirror (y(lo) ↔ y(hi)); acceptance **RV-1** (toggle mirrors the first
  and last candle positions, badge visible), **RV-2** (readout shows the hovered candle's real OHLC; body heights equal in both views), **RV-3**
  (off → identical to the default drawing).

### WP27 — Smooth loading (progressive scan, instant start, delta fetch)

Today the first scan after opening starts from an empty list (a rescan keeps the previous rows until it ends), fetches
every asset (default 100) six at a time and shows all rows only when the last
one is scored, so the screen looks frozen. v2.8:
1. **Instant start:** rows appear at once from the on-device history (`history-store.ts`, up to 10,000 bars per series),
   marked "updating" with their bar time, then refresh in place. Until refreshed by a fresh fetch a row is display-only —
   no ledger row, no paper proposal, no reversal or Radar alert (the v2.2 rule: cached history is never presented or
   recorded as a live signal); a row whose fetch fails says "not updated" with its bar time.
2. **Priority order:** the user's watchlist/selection, otherwise the top 10 by volume, scored on **all timeframes**; then
   the next 20, then the next 20 … up to the chosen list size (default 100); **200 only when the user selects "show
   all"**. Rows appear as each one is scored (batched UI updates, at most every 250 ms); cross-sectional fields (RS percentile,
   BTC beta, universe rank, Fund rank) and ledger rows are computed once the chosen list is complete, as in a full scan,
   so recorded rows equal today's; the other timeframes of the priority rows are cached for instant switching and
   recorded only when that timeframe is scanned.
3. **Delta fetch** (extends the v2.1.1 warm start, which asks 200 bars once this session has seen 1,000 contiguous
   bars; `clampLimit` sets a 100-bar minimum today): when stored history is contiguous, request only the bars after the last stored closed bar (plus one
   overlap bar to verify continuity; if the overlap bar differs from the stored one, the stored series is discarded);
   otherwise the full window. Engine inputs are unchanged (same 1,000-bar window), so
   scores equal a full fetch (test).
4. Cancel/resume: a new source or timeframe cancels queued work; the existing progress bar shows overall progress.
- **Tests:** first rows render before the scan completes; delta-fetched series equal full-fetched series; pacing and
  429/418 halt still respected; DS-1 unchanged at rest.

### WP28 — Encrypted cloud backup (Google Drive, OneDrive, pCloud, Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2)

**Off by default** (Settings → Backup, collapsed). Backs up the user's own records — ledger, paper journal, settings,
pre-registrations, scoring profiles. Market history stays on the device (re-downloadable for free).
- **Providers:** Google Drive (app-data folder scope), Microsoft OneDrive (app folder, `Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder`),
  pCloud (its browser OAuth flow; region host taken from the sign-in response, accepted only if it is `api.pcloud.com` or `eapi.pcloud.com`, both
  listed in CSP/R8) — signed in from the app with
  PKCE/browser flows, **no client secret in the build**, no third-party sign-in script (R6/CSP) — OneDrive: SPA code +
  PKCE (no secret; the refresh token lasts 24 h, so a web backup may ask to sign in again); pCloud: token flow; Google:
  on desktop the Desktop-app client with loopback + PKCE (its client_secret is optional); on the web Google offers only
  its sign-in script or a legacy implicit endpoint it calls insecure, so Google is shown as "desktop only" on the web
  unless the build verifies a script-free code + PKCE flow without a secret [builder-check call]; Backblaze B2 and
  Cloudflare R2 — S3-style access keys, so **desktop only through the local gateway** (keys never leave the PC).
- **Build guard:** the backup is encrypted on the device before upload with **AES-256-GCM** (WebCrypto), key derived from
  the user's passphrase with **PBKDF2-SHA-256** (iteration count per current OWASP guidance, recorded in ADR-0019); the
  GCM tag authenticates it (salt, iteration count, schema version and backup time in the authenticated header), so a
  changed file fails to open; an older backup made with the same passphrase still opens, so restore shows its backup
  time and asks before replacing newer records. Restore parses **plain JSON only** (no `eval`, no
  HTML, no functions), validates the schema version, field types and sizes (≤ 20 MB), and never executes anything;
  passphrase never stored or uploaded; forgetting it means the backup cannot be opened (the guide says so).
- **Sign-in in the desktop and phone apps:** the OAuth redirect needs a loopback address (desktop) or app link (phone),
  handled in `packages/adapters`; verified per platform at build; where a platform cannot complete sign-in, that
  provider is shown as "web/desktop only".
- **Hosts:** each provider's sign-in and storage hosts — ADR-0003 amendment, CSP, egress manifest, R8; B2/R2 hosts in the
  gateway only.
- **Tests:** round-trip (encrypt → upload mock → download → decrypt) equals the original; a flipped byte fails; a wrong
  passphrase fails; an oversize or schema-invalid file is rejected without side effects.
