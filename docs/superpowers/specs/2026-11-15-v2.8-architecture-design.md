## 1. Intent

Ronald uses the Terminal to judge crypto setups and practise in paper trading. The audits showed the app *measures* edge
like a professional but *builds* signals like a retail dashboard, has softer loss limits than any prop firm, and shows
pattern "bias" that is mostly noise. v2.8 makes it honest and safe first, then lets him **see** the difference between
ordinary indicators and how trend funds trade, and **see** possible next moves as simulated candles with odds and
reasons — without changing the screens he already knows.

**Success:** (1) at 10% below its peak the paper account stops trading and closes its trades (only a price gap can take
it further), and no lock can be loosened while it is active;
(2) the Pattern column abstains on noise; (3) Fund trend and Scenarios are one tap away and clearly labelled "not a
forecast"; (4) the ledger can later tell whether Scenarios' odds and range bands were right (coverage, Brier score);
(5) with default settings the app looks exactly like v2.7.

**Anti-goals (a failure even if it works):** simulations that read as predictions; anything that blinks without a way to
stop it; a changed default layout; new voting indicators; any AI-written text in the explanations; a new network host.

## WP19 — Safety and honesty fixes (strategy audit)

Paper risk (`risk-engine.ts`, `paper-broker.ts`):
- **Drawdown lock (owner decision 15 Nov):** `RiskSettings.maxDrawdownLockPct` (default 10, range 1–50).
  `AccountState.peakEquity` = highest equity incl. open P&L, updated whenever positions are marked (every scan; bars
  missed while the app was closed are marked on the next scan from their real closes). When equity ≤ peak × (1 − pct) the
  lock (a) blocks new trades (`paper.rule.drawdownLock`) and (b) **closes every open trade at the next bar's open**,
  reason "drawdown lock" (gaps can still take the loss past 10%; the guide says so). Typed **RESET** (same word as the kill
  switch, separate button) sets the peak to current equity — a fresh 10% — and is journaled; the no-loosening rule starts
  again from the next UTC day. "Start of day/week" equity = the first mark at or after 00:00 UTC Monday / each day.
- **Locks can't be loosened while active:** `updateSettings` refuses increases to loss locks, risk per trade, open risk,
  leverage while any lock is active (`invalid: ["locked"]`); allowed again from the next UTC day.
- **Costs inside the risk budget:** size = budget ÷ (stop distance + entry × round-trip cost), round-trip = 2 × (taker fee +
  slippage); a clean stop-out ≈ −1 R. Docs say a gap can still lose more.
- **Open losses count** in daily/weekly locks (equity vs start-of-day / start-of-week equity).
- **Max drawdown %** fixed in `journalStats` (largest % drawdown, tracked separately).
- **Event blackout** (default on): 2 h before to 1 h after FOMC/CPI/NFP → `paper.rule.event`; `macro-calendar.ts`
  exports `coverageUntil(kind)` and the existing macro banner warns when any kind has < 30 days of dates left
  (`MACRO_CALENDAR_UNTIL` removed); the 2027 dates are added in WP19 so the warning is not shown on release day. Calendar data extended through 2027 from the Fed/BLS/ECB pages (sources recorded; dates the session cannot
  fetch are marked unverified and not used for blocking).
- **Correlated crypto cap:** `maxCryptoPositions` (default 3).
- **Depeg gate:** block when the quote's depeg level is ≥ 1%.
- **Time stop:** setting, **off by default** (preset 24 bars when turned on) — on by default would change every paper
  trade's R and the ADR-0010 comparison [builder-check call]; exit at the next open, reason "time".
- **Measured slippage** per pair in paper fills where the ledger has it.
- **Intraday as context:** proposals from 1m–15m blocked by default (`paper.rule.intradayContext`, setting to allow).
- **Warnings:** risk per trade > 1%; leverage capped at 1 until liquidation is modelled.
- **Journal:** profit factor, Wilson range on win rate, R histogram, "too few trades to judge" below 30, MAE/MFE per trade,
  pre-trade reason + setup tag at approval, tags/notes in CSV (labels localised).

Signals and wording:
- **Pattern engine (H1):** `MIN_MATCHES 20`, `MAX_MATCHES 40`, `MAX_HISTORY_ANCHORS 600`; call a direction only when the
  Wilson lower bound exceeds base rate + 0.02; confidence from (lower bound − base rate). UI strings move to i18n:
  "Historical analogues (descriptive)", "Past analogues ↑/↓", "No excess over base rate". Same column, same place.
- **FVG:** minimum gap 0.5 × ATR(14); label "FVG adjustment (unvalidated)".
- **Wording:** "Reconciled" → "Closed paper trades … reconciliation is a separate step"; "strong signals become proposals"
  → "STRONG score bands become practice proposals (not yet validated by the ledger)"; power expectations (years, not
  weeks); macro "less reliable" → "volatility is often higher… not yet measured"; reversal HIGH = clue count;
  `paper-trading.md` brought to v2.8.
- **Tests:** production-path noise regression (Gaussian, t3, GARCH; 1h/4h/1d; ≤ 7% labelled, ≤ 2% STRONG); test M3
  tightened to ≤ 6%; Pattern abstains on noise (≥ 95% NONE, 0 HIGH); every paper rule above.
- **Engine:** these change engine behaviour → `ENGINE_VERSION 2.4.0`, new config id pinned in `measure.test.mjs`. Ledger
  rows of 2.3.0 stay and are reported separately (existing mechanism).

## WP21 — Fund trend (overlay, ribbon, panel, rank)

**Method** (Moskowitz–Ooi–Pedersen 2012; Hurst–Ooi–Pedersen 2017), on **closed daily bars**:
- returns over 30, 91 and 365 days (crypto trades every day; ≈ 1, 3, 12 months);
- daily volatility σ: EWMA of squared daily log returns, centre of mass 60 days;
- per horizon k: zₖ = ln(Cₜ / Cₜ₋ₖ) / (σ·√k), clipped to ±2; **Fund trend** = 50 × mean(zₖ) → −100…+100;
- **Fund size** = min(200%, 40% ÷ annualised σ) — what a fund targeting 40% yearly volatility per asset would hold,
  as % of normal size;
- needs ≥ 366 closed daily bars, else "not enough history"; daily klines fetched from the row's own source (same hosts,
  limit 1,000, weight 2), cached per UTC day per pair, closed bars only;
- **which rows [builder-check call]:** crypto rows only (spot, perps and Coins.ph peso pairs from their own daily bars);
  tokenised stocks, FX and commodities show "n/a" (their weekend prices are synthetic). The value does not depend on the
  row's timeframe. Budget: one daily request per crypto pair per UTC day (≈ 100 × weight 2 = 200 of 6,000/min on
  Binance), queued behind the scan with the existing pacing and 429/418 halt; the recorder does the same.
- Intraday bars take the value computed from daily bars **closed before** that bar's day (no look-ahead).

**On screen** (all off by default):
- **Ribbon** (Indicators menu): thin band at the bottom of the price pane; green up / red down / grey |x| < 15; opacity
  rises with |x|.
- **Panel** (Indicators menu): Confluence score and Fund trend on one −100…+100 axis; Fund size as a shaded area.
  **Per-bar score [builder-check call]:** the visible bars (at most 300) are re-scored in the report worker with the
  production 1,000-bar window (the catch-up path already does this), cached per pair, timeframe and last bar; ledger rows
  are reused where they exist. So the line and the ◆ markers use exactly the list's score. Bars beyond 300 show no line.
- **Disagreement:** Confluence BUY/SELL (|score| ≥ 55) against a Fund trend of the opposite sign with |x| ≥ 25 →
  diagonal hatching on those bars + ◆ marker; list row gets ◆ and a thin outline **only when the ribbon is on**.
- **Pulse:** new disagreements pulse 3× over ~3 s then stay (default); Off; Keep pulsing (≤ 1 pulse/s, until the coin
  is opened, with a stop button). `prefers-reduced-motion` → no animation.
- **Fund rank** (inside the Fund trend panel and Scenarios; optional list column, off): rank of the coin's Fund trend
  across the scanned crypto rows.
- **Ledger:** Fund trend value recorded per row (`ft`) as a benchmark; ledger report adds "Fund trend sign" as a
  benchmark next to the existing 20-bar momentum.

## WP22 — Scenarios (simulation with candlesticks)

New pure modules `lib/scenarios.ts` (simulation, scenario odds) and `lib/scenario-explain.ts` (rule-based text); run in the
existing report worker; rendered by `components/scenario-panel.tsx` and a new optional `projection` prop of `PriceChart`.

**History used:** the row's own closed bars on the current timeframe (the 1,000 the sheet has, plus any loaded with
"Load 5K"). Minimum 300 bars, else the section says "not enough history".

**Candle bootstrap (the simulation):**
- each past candle becomes a relative candle (open, high, low, close ÷ previous close);
- **200 paths**, each H steps, built by a **block bootstrap** (blocks of 6 candles, so calm and wild stretches stay
  together), drawing blocks from bars whose volatility (ATR(14)/close percentile) is within ±15 points of today's;
- **direction-neutral:** the sample's mean log return is removed, so the fan says how far, never which way;
- horizons: **6 bars** (matches the Pattern and ledger horizon) and **24 bars**, switchable;
- **reproducible:** seeded from pair + timeframe + last closed bar time (same view → same simulation);
- **range bands** at each step: 2.5–97.5% (light) and 16–84% (darker) of simulated closes; median dotted.

**Three scenarios (odds from the coin's own past):**
- **state [builder-check call — with 1,000 bars there are only ~165 non-overlapping 6-bar windows]:** the Fund trend
  direction only (**down ≤ −15 · flat · up ≥ 15**); volatility already shapes the range bands, so it is not crossed in;
- matches: past bars in that state, sampled every 2 bars (overlapping windows allowed); the **effective sample**
  n_eff = matches × 2 ÷ H must be **≥ 20**. If not, fall back to **all bars** (the base rate itself) and say so
  ("not enough similar moments — showing this coin's overall odds"). At H = 24 this usually needs "Load 5K";
- outcome after H bars, in units of σ_H (ATR-based): **Continue** > +0.5 in the trend's direction, **Reverse** < −0.5,
  **Pause** otherwise; when the trend is flat: **Up / Range / Down**;
- shown as % with Wilson 95% range, number of cases, and the coin's **all-bars base rate** beside it;
- **scenario path:** among the 200 simulated paths whose end falls in that scenario's bucket, the one nearest the bucket
  median; if a bucket has no simulated path, no candles are drawn for it (the odds still show).

**Graphic (only after the user opens the section and taps "Show on chart"):**
- the chart's time axis extends by H empty slots to the right of the last real candle; bands fill them;
- the selected scenario's candles are **hollow with a dashed outline**, a "SIMULATED" label sits on the area, the axis
  marks "now"; tabs **Continue / Pause / Reverse** switch paths;
- **▶ Play** draws the candles one at a time (~250 ms each), stops at H; Pause/Replay; reduced motion → all at once;
- works in zoom, pan, fullscreen and with **Heikin-Ashi** (simulated candles converted the same way);
- turning it off restores the exact v2.7 chart.

**Explanation (rule-based, i18n keys with parameters, 18 languages):** one short paragraph built from: Fund trend
state; Confluence label and which bloc disagrees; volatility regime; the invalidation level (2 × ATR(14) against the
trend, or the last swing high/low over 20 bars, whichever is nearer); the next macro event inside the horizon; funding
/ basis when the row is a perp; and the sample-size caveat. Fixed header: **"Possible scenarios from past data — not a
forecast."**

**Which run is official [builder-check call]:** the scan path (`scan-record.ts`, fixed 1,000-bar window, **H = 6**) is
the official one; the recorder and catch-up rows compute the same fields from the same data. The sheet uses its own
history (incl. "Load 5K") and says "the recorded check uses the last 1,000 bars" when they differ.

**Self-check (ledger):** each scanned row records the 6-bar band edges (16/84 and 2.5/97.5) and the three scenario
probabilities (`scb`, `scp` fields; CSV columns + sanitiser). When outcomes close, a collapsed **Scenario check**
section in the ledger dialog shows band coverage (target 68% / 95%, with Wilson range) and the Brier score of the
scenario odds against the base-rate odds.

**Heikin-Ashi** (Indicators menu, display only, default off): HA candles for real and simulated bars; scores, levels
and the readout keep using real prices (readout shows both).

## WP24 — Chart reflect view (display only)

**Why:** a falling market is harder to read than a rising one for many people; traders check a chart's symmetry by flipping
it (TradingView calls this "invert scale"). **What:** an *Indicators* menu toggle **Reflect view** (default off; keyboard
**I** on PC) that mirrors the price pane vertically: the price axis runs high-to-low, so a fall is drawn as a rise.
- Candles keep their **real colours and real prices** (a falling candle stays red; axis labels and the readout show
  real prices in their real order of value); only the drawing is mirrored. MACD and volume panels are not flipped; the
  Fund trend panel is flipped together with price so the two stay comparable.
- A clear **"Reflected"** badge sits on the price pane (and in fullscreen and screenshots), so a reflected chart is
  never mistaken for a real uptrend; the toggle remembers its state per device like the other layers.
- Works with zoom, pan, fullscreen, Heikin-Ashi, the Fund trend ribbon and the Scenarios simulation (simulated candles
  and bands are mirrored the same way); turning it off restores the exact v2.7 drawing.
- Implementation: one y-mapping function in `price-chart.tsx` (`y = reflect ? PAD_T + (price − lo)·k : PAD_T + (hi −
  price)·k`) used by every price-pane layer, so no layer can be missed; the crosshair/readout maps back to real price.
- Tests: unit test that the mapping is an exact mirror (y(lo) ↔ y(hi)); acceptance **RV-1** (toggle mirrors the first
  and last candle positions, badge visible), **RV-2** (readout shows the real price under the cursor), **RV-3**
  (off → identical to the default drawing).

## WP23 — Confluence v2 (challenger beside Classic)

A second engine profile, scored in the same scan and recorded under its own config id, never shown in the list:
volatility-standardised inputs (each indicator's continuous measure — e.g. price − MA, MACD histogram, band position —
÷ ATR(14) or its own rolling σ, clipped ±1; the per-indicator mapping is recorded in a table in ADR-0017), hysteresis (enter |score| ≥ 55, return to
NEUTRAL below 40), the four contrarian oscillators dropped on ≥ 4h, bands re-calibrated on noise to the same targets as Classic (≤ 7% labelled, ≤ 2% STRONG). Optional detail-sheet
line "Confluence v2 (test)" when the setting is on. The ledger compares Classic and v2 head-to-head; nothing switches over
without a later owner decision based on that record.
