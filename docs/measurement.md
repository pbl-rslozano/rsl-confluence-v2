# Measuring the signals — 2.3.0

The terminal now records every signal it produces and measures what happened next. Use this before trusting, sizing or automating anything.

## What is recorded (signal ledger)
- One row per **series × closed bar × engine**: source, timeframe, pair, asset class, bar time, close, score, direction, strength, suppressed / low-volatility flags, valid voters, bull/bear counts, efficiency ratio, the four bloc scores, universe rank, 24 h quote volume, reversal direction/confidence, **warm-up bars** and the **engine id**.
- **Engine id (`configId`)**: 16-hex hash of the engine version, its parameters and its *behaviour* on a fixed test series. Any change to an indicator, band, weight or window changes it, so results from different engines are never pooled. It is identical in the browser bundle and in Node (acceptance M-8b; unit test pins `5c30b5c39f5ba3f2` for engine 2.4.0; 2.3.0 was `2c1a18ad8a61e9ab`).
- **Catch-up**: when the app opens, the scan worker scores the bars that closed since the last recorded bar **of each series** (v2.3: one cursor per source × timeframe × pair; at most 48 bars per series, full 1,000-bar windows only). A pair seen for the **first** time starts forward from now: back-filling bars from before it entered the scan would add selection look-ahead (review C6). A failed catch-up job, or a storage-full write, never advances the cursor, so the hole is retried; holes longer than 48 bars are logged as **recording gaps** (shown in the dialog). Catch-up rows carry the rank at catch-up time (`rank_source = now`), never an invented historical rank. The headless recorder follows the same rules.
- **Universe log (v2.3)**: the **full unfiltered venue ranking** (top 300 by 24 h quote volume, every class) is stored once per source × bar — only for the scan's newest bar — and every row stores `universe_rank_full`. It counts toward the row cap (≈ 40 rows each) and is pruned with its day. Survivorship and rank look-ahead can therefore be checked later from the ledger itself.
- **Context fields (v2.3, logged, never scored)**: cross-sectional relative-strength percentile, 100-bar beta to BTC, higher-timeframe trend (EMA-20 slope on 4× bars), taker imbalance (Binance spot), hours to the nearest macro event, perp basis and band-pinned flag, stock split flag, earnings window (manual list, ships empty). They let you test "does it work better when …?" later without re-collecting. Token unlock schedules have no keyless public source and are not logged.
- **v2.8 (WP19) — ledger completeness (logged, never scored)**. Rows recorded before v2.8, and any field not available, are `null` (also after import).
  - **`vt` — the 18 individual votes**, base-4 packed into one integer 0 … 4¹⁸ − 1 (68 719 476 735). That is 36 bits — safe in a JS number but beyond 32-bit shifts — so it is packed and unpacked with **× 4 / % 4 only** (`packVotes` / `unpackVotes` in `ledger-metrics.ts`). **Digit order = the engine's indicator order**, digit 0 the least significant: 0 SMA 20/50 · 1 EMA 9/21 · 2 MACD 12/26/9 · 3 ADX 14 · 4 Parabolic SAR · 5 Ichimoku · 6 Supertrend 10/3 · 7 RSI 14 · 8 Stochastic 14/3 · 9 CCI 20 · 10 Williams %R 14 · 11 Awesome Osc. · 12 MFI 14 · 13 Bollinger 20/2 · 14 Keltner 20/10 · 15 VWAP · 16 OBV · 17 CMF 20 (`VOTING_SLOT_IDS` in `indicators.ts`; ATR 14, Std Dev 20 and FVG are informational and not in the vector). **Digit values**: 0 bullish, 1 bearish, 2 neutral, 3 not valid. The indicator's `valid` flag is read first — an invalid indicator reports state NEUTRAL but is written 3 — and a voter the scoring profile disabled (`informational`) is also written 3. A row without indicators (stock-split mute) is all 3s. Decode: digit *i* = ⌊vt / 4ⁱ⌋ mod 4.
  - **`pd`, `pc` — the Pattern output** (historical analogues) of that scan: direction UP 1 / DOWN −1 / NONE 0; confidence none 0 / low 1 / moderate 2 / high 3. Live rows in the app and the recorder; catch-up and replay rows do not compute it (`null`).
  - **`fr`, `oiChg` — funding rate and open-interest change** from the recorder's derivative snapshots (Bybit linear; open interest for its top 10 only): `fr` = funding per interval (fraction) of the newest snapshot, `oiChg` = % change between the two newest distinct open-interest readings — only snapshots taken **at or before the bar close and at most 2 h before it** (a later one would be look-ahead). **When they are populated**: only when the recorder records derivatives (`--derivs on`, or `auto` with `--source bybit-perp`), for pairs with a valid Bybit linear funding print (`oiChg` only for the recorder's top-10 open-interest symbols, and only once two distinct hourly readings exist). On bars of 1 h or less every cycle's snapshot is inside the window. On longer bars with the default once-per-bar cadence the looping recorder takes a **derivatives-only snapshot 15 min before each close** (no candles, no scoring, no ledger rows; same Bybit hosts and pacing, skipped during a rate-limit cooldown), so the next rows carry them; the first rows after a start carry `null` (the start-up snapshot is after their close). A recorder run only with `--once` from an external scheduler at each close never takes that pre-close snapshot, so its rows carry `null` unless `--interval-seconds` ≤ 7200 or another run snapshots inside the window. Catch-up rows use the snapshots before their own close. The recorder keeps recent snapshots in memory and reloads the derivs log tail on start. **App rows** carry them only from recorder snapshots imported in the derivatives panel (and only for bars whose close is ≤ 2 h after an imported snapshot); otherwise `null`.
  - **`ft` — Fund trend** (v2.8, WP21), −100…+100 to one decimal, a benchmark that is **never scored**: crypto rows only, computed from the pair's closed daily bars whose close time is at or before the row's own bar close (no look-ahead). Catch-up rows and live rows, in the app and in the recorder, use one rule (`applyFundTrend`): the daily bars closed at or before **the row's own bar close**, never the value "now", so a 1w or 1M row does not see the days after its bar. Live rows are written once this scan's daily-bar feed has settled (at most 90 s later, or at once if the scan is superseded) and then take their value from the daily bars held at that moment; a row whose newest usable daily bar closed more than 36 h before the row's close, or whose pair has no daily bars (a halt, a timeout, or a build that cannot fetch them), gets `null`; a halt after a 429, a pair still loading or too little history (< 366 daily bars) logs `null` = not available at the time). Non-crypto rows and older rows have no `ft`. The report's benchmarks add **Fund trend sign** next to the 20-bar momentum: long when `ft` > 0, short when `ft` < 0, rows with `ft` null or 0 excluded from n, same costs and thinning as the momentum benchmark. The import range-checks it (|`ft`| ≤ 100, formula-like or non-numeric rejects the whole row); the CSV column `fund_trend` follows the WP19 ones, so a v2.7 or earlier v2.8 CSV imports with `ft` null.
  - CSV columns appended after the v2.7 ones: `vote_vector`, `pattern_dir`, `pattern_conf`, `funding_rate`, `oi_change_pct`. The ledger import (JSON, JSONL **and, since v2.8, CSV**) type- and range-checks every new field (`vt` an integer 0 … 4¹⁸ − 1, `pd` −1/0/1, `pc` 0–3, |`fr`| ≤ 0.05, −100 ≤ `oiChg` ≤ 10 000); a value out of range or formula-like (`=1+1`) rejects the whole row. A v2.7 CSV without the new columns imports with them `null`.
  - **`rad` — market-radar context** (v2.8, WP25), logged, **never scored** (no alert blocks or places a trade): the radar's BTC values at the row's bar close — `frp` funding-rate 30-day percentile (0–100), `oi24` open-interest change over 24 h (%, −100…10 000), `ls` global long/short account ratio (0–100), `tk` taker-buy share of the newest closed 1 h bar (0–1), `usP` / `krP` / `phP` the US, Korea and Philippines premiums (%, −50…50), `al` the active alert codes (at most 16, each `[A-Z0-9_]{1,32}`), and `t` the context's own time. A row takes the **newest context at or before its own bar close, at most 2 h old** (never a later one); `rad` is `null` when **Record radar context** (Scoring profile popover, default off) or the recorder's `--radar` is off, or no entry qualifies; each field is `null` when its source had no current value. In the app the context log is the per-device one the radar writes every 5 min while recording is on (the panel may stay closed); in the recorder it is `<out>/radar/YYYY-MM.jsonl`, refreshed each cycle (at most every 5 minutes) and always at the pre-close wake; a run only with `--once` on bars longer than 2 h has no pre-close wake, so its rows carry `rad` null. CSV: eight columns appended after `fund_trend` — `radar_funding_pctl`, `radar_oi_chg_24h`, `radar_long_short`, `radar_taker_share`, `radar_us_premium`, `radar_kr_premium`, `radar_ph_premium`, `radar_alerts` (codes joined by `|`, empty = none; the WP22 scenario columns follow it). The CSV has no column for the context's own time, so an imported CSV row's `rad.t` is its bar close, and a context whose fields are all empty reads back as `null`. The import range-checks every field (a value out of range, a formula-like cell or an alert code that does not match rejects the whole row); a CSV without the columns imports with `rad` null.
  - **`scb`, `scp` — scenario fields** (v2.8, WP22), logged, **never scored**, from the **official run**: the 1,000 bars ending at the row's own bar, horizon H = 6, the same code as the detail sheet (`scenarioFields`) in the scan, the catch-up and the recorder. The Fund trend state of the signal bar is the row's `ft`; earlier bars use the daily bars closed at or before their own close (crypto with a daily source only, otherwise unknown).
    - **`scb`** = four numbers, in order **[lo95, lo68, hi68, hi95]**: the simulated close at step 6 at the 2.5 %, 16 %, 84 % and 97.5 % quantiles, as the natural log of the band divided by the signal close, rounded to 5 decimals (log-return units, ascending). `null` when the window has fewer than 300 bars, the bar is not in the stored bars, or a value is not finite.
    - **`scp`** = `{s, p, b, w, f}`: `s` the Fund trend state (−1 down, 0 flat, 1 up, `null` unknown); `p` = [up, range, down] odds of the 6-bar outcome (buckets at ±0.5 σ_6), 3 decimals, summing to 1 ± 0.01; `b` = the coin's all-bars base rates in the same order, 3 decimals; `w` = σ_6 = ATR(14)/close × √6 at the signal bar, 5 decimals, in (0, 5]; `f` = 1 when the overall odds were used (no state, or fewer than 20 effective cases), else 0. For a down state, "continue" is the down bucket; the ledger keeps the plain up / range / down order.
    - **Null cases.** Rows recorded before v2.8 Step 5, rows with fewer than 300 bars, and any window the simulation cannot run on have both fields `null`. The writer never stores a value the import would reject: `scb` is `null` when an edge is beyond ±5, and `scp` is `null` when `w` > 5 (possible right after a crash of 90 % or more); a row whose scenario work fails on bad bars gets both `null` and is still written. The import is strict: a value out of range, not finite, a band not ascending, probabilities that do not sum to 1, or a formula-like cell rejects the whole row; unknown `scp` keys are dropped; a CSV without the columns imports with both `null`.
    - **CSV**: 13 columns after `radar_alerts`: `scenario_band_lo95`, `scenario_band_lo68`, `scenario_band_hi68`, `scenario_band_hi95`, `scenario_state`, `scenario_p_up`, `scenario_p_range`, `scenario_p_down`, `scenario_base_up`, `scenario_base_range`, `scenario_base_down`, `scenario_width`, `scenario_fallback`. All empty cells read back as `null`; a partial set rejects the row.
  - **Scenario check (method).** Shown collapsed at the end of the ledger dialog. For each series (source × timeframe × pair) it keeps **non-overlapping 6-bar windows** (the first row, then the next one at least 6 bars later), measures the realised return ln(close at t+6 ÷ signal close) from the stored bars (null across a hole or before the horizon closes) and uses only rows that also pass the ledger's own usable-row filter (full-window or ≥ 300-bar rows per the toggle, open markets), so closed-market rows and rows under the bar-count minimum are not checked. **Band coverage** = the share of windows whose return lies inside [lo68, hi68] and [lo95, hi95], with a Wilson 95 % range; the targets are 68 % and 95 %. **Brier score** (three outcomes, sum over outcomes of (p − outcome)²), averaged over windows whose `scp.f` = 0, for the scenario odds `p` and for the base-rate odds `b`, with the realised outcome bucketed as return ÷ `w` at ±0.5. Rows that fell back to the overall odds are skipped (their `p` equals `b`). Lower is better; scenarios that do not beat the base rate add nothing. Rows whose bars are no longer stored are listed as not measured.
  - **`v2` — the Confluence v2 challenger** (v2.8, WP23, ADR-0017), logged, **never scored**: `{cfg, s, m, st}` on the Classic row, about 54 bytes per row (a stored `"v2":null` is 10), with one shared outcome per row.
    - **Members.** `cfg` = the v2 config id (16 hex; `f5c94871f16f6673` for v2 `cv2-1.0.0`, gain 0.8, STRONG band 64); `s` = the v2 score, −100…100, or `null` when v2 is suppressed (no score at the bar: fewer than 13 of its 18 inputs valid, or a stock split); `m` = the hysteresis label (−1 SELL, 0 NEUTRAL, 1 BUY); `st` = strength (0 weak, 1 moderate, 2 strong).
    - **Null cases.** The whole field `v2` is `null` or absent for rows recorded before WP23, for a window under the engine's bar minimum (`scoreV2` is not usable there, so `v2Compact` returns null) and for a row where v2 faulted (`guardedV2` returns null; v2 runs behind its own guard, so Classic's fields are never affected). These rows stay valid and are the ones counted as "not compared".
    - **Suppressed, not null.** A usable window with no v2 score (a stock split guard, or fewer than 13 of the 18 inputs valid, for example without usable volume) is stored as `{cfg, s: null, m: 0, st: 0}`. It is a v2 row: `asV2Row` turns it into a suppressed row (`sup` 1), the head-to-head keeps it and it is compared as no signal, not as "not compared".
    - **Consistency rules.** The import is strict (the `scb`/`scp` pattern): `cfg` must be 16 hex, `s` an integer in −100…100 or `null`, `m` −1/0/1, `st` 0/1/2; a hostile or partial value rejects the whole row, unknown keys are dropped. `s` `null` goes with `m` 0 and `st` 0, and `m` is 0 exactly when `st` is 0. The label is derived from the row's own window (the last 300 per-bar scores, starting NEUTRAL), never from stored state, so every path that scores the same bars writes the same `v2`; recorded rows call `scoreV2` on the row's own 1,000-bar window.
    - **CSV.** Four columns after `scenario_fallback`: `v2_config_id`, `v2_score`, `v2_direction`, `v2_strength` (empty when null; a CSV without them imports with `v2` null; all four empty reads back as `null`, a partial set is rejected). The `net_return_h*` columns, when present, follow them.
    - **Head-to-head (`head-to-head.ts`).** `asV2Row(row)` is the same row with v2's score, label and strength (null when the row has no v2). `pairedOutcomes` keeps only the outcomes of rows that carry v2, so both engines are measured on the same bars; `retargetOutcome` re-prices the row's one measured outcome for v2's direction: gross, cost and funding are shared, only the direction, and so the net, changes (NEUTRAL is measured long, as for Classic; the excursions are not compared). **Label churn** = label changes per 100 consecutive bar pairs, per source × timeframe × pair series; a step is a pair of rows at most one bar apart (a missing bar breaks it). `sideOf` pools BUY · all and SELL · all, weighted by n, into the line the ledger section and the replay print: signals, hit rate, mean net and edge in bp, IC t, PSR and churn. Classic's report, gate and pre-registration never read `v2`.
    - **Evidence.** `docs/research/evidence/wp23-classic-baseline-2026-11-17.json` (Classic before WP23), `wp23-classic-vs-v2-2026-11-18.{json,md}` (Classic before and after, unchanged, and v2's first metrics on the existing fixtures, synthetic and noise-like), `wp23-v2-calibration-2026-11-18.json` (gain and band) and `wp23-v2-scaling-diagnostic-2026-11-18.json` (why the contrarian bloc is kept). Small samples are labelled in the tables; none of it is a claim of edge.
    - **Contrarian oscillators.** RSI, Stochastic, Williams %R and MFI are kept on every interval, including 4h and above (owner decision, 18 Nov 2026; this supersedes the spec's drop on ≥ 4h).
    - **Recorder and replay.** Both record `v2` with the app's own code (`docs/recorder.md`); the replay has `--no-v2` (the four columns stay empty) and prints a head-to-head table per horizon. Catch-up sends its jobs to the worker in chunks of at most 25.
- Symbols whose fetch failed in a scan are shown as "feed unavailable" and are **not** scored or recorded.
- The report only pools rows from the **current engine id**, source, timeframe and asset class; rows from other engines are counted but never mixed in.
- Stored as one small document per bar (all pairs of that bar) with day indexes for pruning: native SQLite on desktop/mobile; in the browser a **separate IndexedDB database** (`rsl-conf-ledger`), so the main database never needs an upgrade. Kept 400 days on durable storage (row cap 1 M on SQLite, 400 k on IndexedDB), 14 days / 5 k rows on localStorage, 2 days in memory-only mode. If storage fills up, the oldest quarter of days is dropped and recording continues. **Not part of `.rslbak` backups** — use Export CSV/JSON.

## How outcomes are measured (no look-ahead)
- Entry: **open of the next bar** after the signal bar closes. Exit: close of bar *t + h*, for h = 1, 3, 6, 12, 24.
- Net return = direction × gross − 2 × (fee + slippage) − funding (perpetuals; longs pay, shorts receive).
- Default costs (base tier, taker, Oct 2026): spot 10 bp fee; Bybit linear 5.5 bp; Bitget perps 6 bp; slippage 5 bp placeholder **plus a per-class extra** (tokenised stocks and RWAs +10 bp, commodities +5, FX +3); perp funding 1 bp per 8 h (0.01 %). Base rates include the same funding, so perps are not biased toward shorts.
- **Measured slippage (v2.3)**: once a day the app reads the public order book of the top 10 pairs and stores the one-side slippage of a $1,000 market order (half spread + book walk). Where a pair has a measurement it replaces the placeholder and class extra.
- **Outcome snapshots (v2.3)**: once measured, each outcome (gross, MFE, MAE, hours held) is stored next to its bar, so a delisted pair or a cleared candle cache does not lose its results. Fees, slippage **and funding** are re-priced from today's cost model when a snapshot is used. A pair that left the scan before its horizons closed gets its missing bars fetched once from public history when the dialog opens (≤ 10 pairs per open); the recorder keeps fetching candles for such pairs until their longest horizon has closed.
- **Stock splits (v2.3)**: no outcome is measured across a probable split (it would read as a −50 % or +100 % trade).
- No outcome is computed across a hole in the data, or before the horizon has closed.
- **Primary statistics use full-window rows only (1,000 bars the engine actually saw, v2.3)**; a toggle shows ≥ 300-bar rows (more data, some warm-up bias). Session-filtered stocks never reach 1,000 bars, and neither do 2d/4d/1w/1M or very young series; the first scan of a new series yields 999 closed bars (Binance returns at most 1,000 including the bar in progress), so it becomes full-window from its second scan.
- **Costs are matched (v2.3, review C1)**: base rates, the momentum benchmark, the shuffle replicas and the 2× check are charged the **same cost the compared signals paid**, so a signal that only fires on cheap pairs cannot show "edge" that is just a cost difference.

## What the report shows (toolbar → Ledger)
| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
Overlap control (v2.2): every bar is recorded, so outcomes at horizon *h* overlap by *h − 1* bars. All figures therefore use **non-overlapping outcomes per series** (a signal inside an open holding window is skipped), the PSR uses **one portfolio return per block of h bars**, and the rank IC uses **cross-sections at least h bars apart** (each with ≥ 5 pairs — a replay needs at least 5 symbols for an IC). On pure noise the gate's PSR now passes ~3 % of windows instead of ~50 % (unit-tested).

| Hit rate vs **Base** | Share of signals with net > 0 vs the share of *all scored bars* (incl. NEUTRAL) that would have beaten costs in the same direction |
| Mean net, **Edge vs base** (bp) | Expectancy after costs, and what the signal adds over holding blindly |
| Profit factor, t | Gross wins ÷ gross losses; t-statistic of the mean |
| Rank IC | Spearman correlation of score vs forward return across all pairs at the same bar, averaged over bars, with t-stat |
| PSR | Probabilistic Sharpe ratio (Bailey & López de Prado) that the true per-trade Sharpe is > 0 |
| Calibration | Mean forward move per score band — a working score should rise monotonically |

| 95 % intervals (v2.3) | Wilson interval of the hit rate (tooltip) and a seeded **block** bootstrap interval of mean net (whole holding periods are resampled, so pairs that move together are not counted as independent) |
| vs BTC (v2.3) | Mean net in excess of BTC's return over the same holding window (buy / sell) |
| vs universe (v2.3) | Mean net in excess of the mean gross return of all scored pairs at the same bar (bars with ≥ 5 pairs) — a score that only leans long in a rising market shows ~0 here |
| Deflated Sharpe (v2.3) | PSR against the expected maximum Sharpe of *N* trials; *N* = distinct variants looked at (source × timeframe × class × window), from the **trials register** |
| Benchmarks (v2.3) | 20-bar momentum on the same bars (edge vs the base in each signal's own direction), the **Fund trend sign** (v2.8, same bars and costs; rows without `ft` excluded) and a **label permutation within each bar**: every BUY/SELL keeps its bar and cost but is moved to a random pair scored on that same bar, 100–200 times; p = share of permutations at least as good. Keeping the bar preserves time clustering, so a market-wide move cannot make random labels look worse than the real ones (an independent draw rejected a true null ~35 % of the time). With one pair per bar the test cannot reject (p ≈ 1) — scan ≥ 5 pairs. |

**Indicative gate** (shown in the dialog): ≥ 200 measured directional signals, positive edge after costs, still positive at **2× costs**, rank-IC t ≥ 2, PSR ≥ 0.95, and since v2.3 **deflated Sharpe ≥ 0.95**, **shuffle p ≤ 0.05**, **beats 20-bar momentum** and a **pre-registered horizon whose decision date has passed**. Passing it is evidence, not proof; it is not a trading recommendation.

**v2.8 (WP19) — measurement honesty**
- **Power readout**: under PSR/DSR the dialog shows *days needed at the current Sharpe* — PSR ≥ 0.95 needs SR·√(n−1) ≥ 1.645·√(1 − γ₃·SR + (γ₄−1)/4·SR²), so n = 1 + (1.645·√(1 − γ₃·SR + (γ₄−1)/4·SR²) / SR)² blocks (term under the root floored at 1e-12, n rounded up). SR, sample skew γ₃ and raw kurtosis γ₄ (3 for normal returns) come from `sharpeStats` on the report's h-bar block returns; n is converted to days at the ledger's observed blocks per day (blocks ÷ days from the first to the last block) and shown as total days and days remaining beyond the blocks already logged. When SR ≤ 0 there is no figure — the current Sharpe never reaches PSR 0.95.
- **Confirmatory gate**: the deflated Sharpe of the *confirmatory* report deflates by the **active pre-registered hypotheses** only — the pre-registrations fixed for the engine id being evaluated (one on an older engine can no longer be confirmed by these rows; at least 1). The *exploratory* report keeps the all-views trial count.
- **Outcome mode `atr-bracket`** (ledger function `measureBracket`, replay `--outcome atr-bracket`): the paper book's bracket — entry at the next bar's open, stop 2 × ATR(14) (Wilder, on the engine window ending at the signal bar), target 2 R, the stop assumed first when a bar touches both, a gap through the stop exits at the open, the same fees, slippage and funding, R = net P&L ÷ (stop distance + reference × round-trip cost). ADR-0010 gate 3 therefore compares paper R with exactly this rule (unit test: the bracket R on a fixture equals `processBars` R). Replay reports n, mean R, hit rate (Wilson 95 %), stops and targets, one trade at a time per pair. The ATR of a live paper proposal is taken on the scan's whole loaded series, so after "Load 5K" it can differ slightly from the engine-window ATR (Wilder's smoothing converges).
- **Rejected proposals** are kept on the paper account (newest 500) with the owner's reason and tag, and their later **bracket outcome** — the same bracket the approval would have used, filled at the first open at or after the rejection — so discretionary choices can be compared with the system.

**Research hygiene (v2.3)**
- **Trials register**: every report variant opened is registered once (source × timeframe × class × window × **each horizon**, since the report shows all horizons); the count over **all engine versions** feeds the deflated Sharpe, so changing the engine does not reset it. Clearing the ledger does **not** reset it. App, replay (`trials.jsonl`) and the MCP server use one record schema (`id`, `at`, `cfg`, `variant`) and one counting rule (distinct variants).
- **Pre-registration**: fix the hypothesis (engine, source, timeframe, class, window, horizon) before looking; the decision date is at least **90 days** later and cannot be moved or rewritten (audited). The gate's pre-registration check only passes **in that view**, and the gate then reads a **confirmatory report built only from rows recorded after the registration** — rows seen before it can never confirm it.
- **Responsiveness**: the report is built in a Web Worker (main-thread fallback); permutation replicas are bounded to ~5 M draws per horizon.
- **Storage durability**: the dialog shows whether browser storage is durable and offers to request it (`navigator.storage.persist()`); native SQLite is always durable.
- **Weekly export**: the dialog reminds you when the last CSV/JSON export is ≥ 7 days old (or never).

## Backtest by replay
Same scoring code, same fixed 1,000-bar window as live:
```
pnpm --filter @rsl/confluence-web replay -- --symbols BTCUSDT,ETHUSDT,SOLUSDT,XRPUSDT,BNBUSDT,ADAUSDT --tf 4h --from 2024-01-01 --oos 2025-07-01
pnpm --filter @rsl/confluence-web replay -- --csv-dir ./binance-klines --tf 1d --from 2022-01-01   # data.binance.vision CSVs
```
Writes `replay-out/ledger.csv`, `report.json`, `report.md` with in-sample vs out-of-sample results (in-sample rows whose longest horizon would reach past `--oos` are embargoed), windows that never span a data gap and a **20-bar momentum baseline** the engine must beat. v2.3 adds:
- a **trials register** (`trials.jsonl` beside the output folder): every distinct run is appended once and the count deflates the Sharpe (`--trials N` overrides);
- **PBO by CSCV** (Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado, Zhu) over the variant grid horizon × {all, strong}, ranking variants by Sharpe on per-bar returns; variants that trade in < 50 % of periods are dropped and only periods where every kept variant traded are used (an empty cell is not a return): ≥ 0.5 means the in-sample choice is likely overfit;
- Wilson / block-bootstrap intervals, BTC and universe excess, within-bar permutation p, per-class costs, `--min-bars` (default 1,000, on the bars the engine saw), Bybit venue types for classification and the context fields — rows identical to live rows for the same bar.

Survivorship-safe data: `node tools/fetch-binance-archive.mjs --list` lists every symbol ever archived on data.binance.vision and marks the **delisted** ones; `--symbols … --tf 4h --from 2022-01 --to 2026-09 --out ./klines` downloads monthly ZIPs, **verifies each against its SHA-256 `.CHECKSUM`** and unpacks the CSV for `--csv-dir`. ## Biases still present — read before believing a number
- **Survivorship**: replay symbols are today's survivors unless you add the delisted pairs listed by `fetch-binance-archive.mjs --list`.
- **Universe look-ahead**: replay rows have no historical top-N rank (rank = 0); forward rows have the universe log.
- **Slippage** is measured only for the top 10 pairs at $10,000 notional (order-book depth, once a day per source); size up and it grows.
- **Multiple testing**: the trials register counts what you look at in the app and in replay — but not what you try elsewhere.
- **Short samples**: a few hundred signals on one timeframe is weak evidence; forward-record 3–6 months before acting. The power readout often shows **years, not weeks**, for a small edge; read it before expecting a verdict.
